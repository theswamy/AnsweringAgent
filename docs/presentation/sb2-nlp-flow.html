How the money moves

NLP is buying $35M of exposure to SB2 from the existing LPs. This is the flow that results — where each dollar of every future exit goes, in what order, and which single number the whole structure still turns on.

What NLP wants

“NLP would like to purchase $35M worth of equity from the ClassB shareholders at a 35% discount — provided they get their 1x back and subsequently participate pari-passu with other ClassB shareholders.” Source document, section S3

Three things are being asked for at once, and each one shapes the flow: a discount (how much they get), 1x back first (when they get it), and pari-passu after (what happens once they are whole).

Step 4 · The pref, and why it is not a round number

You cannot grant a preference over shares you no longer own

NLP's 1x has to come back from two places, because its money went to two places. NLPI owns 12.6% of each position outright — buyers pay it directly, onshore, at every exit, with no preference involved. The preference can only run over the 87.4% SB2 still owns. So it should be sized to the balance:

pref = ( 1 − the share SB2 sold at the portco level ) × $35M

Where it does not close

One percentage sets both the exit split and the pref multiple

The structure sells NLPI 12.6% of each portco position S5, and the tail duly splits each cheque 88 / 12 S10. But the worked exits split it 98.6 / 1.4 — and 1.4% is x1, a percentage of Class B inside the fund, not of a portco position. The same substitution sizes the pref.

If NLPI's onshore slice is…Consistent prefHolding 9.86x anyway
1.4% — the worked exits9.86x · $34.51Mcorrect: NLP whole at exactly $35M
12.6% — the structure8.74x · $30.59Mpref clears only after $39.49M of exits — NLP takes $4.49M more than its 1x, out of Class A and the old LPs
consequence9.86x is not an independently negotiated term — it is a function of the split. Since the exact x1 and x2 are still to be settled, the pref belongs in the documents as a formula or as a cap on aggregate priority receipts, not as a hard multiple on the feeder's $3.5M.

Where it does not close

The preference repays the entity that funded a tenth of it

“Open Question: NLP_F then distributes pro-rata to NLP in India — how does the NLP India fund receive its returns?” Source document, sections S8 and S9 — asked twice

NLPI put in $31.5M. NLPF holds the $34.51M preference that repays it. Nothing in the document connects them — and this is not a detail to paper later: the alternative, repaying NLPI at the portco level through an enlarged onshore slice until it is whole, changes every number in the worked exits, including the pref multiple.

This is the first thing to settle. Everything downstream is conditional on the answer.

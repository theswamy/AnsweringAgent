# Open Loops — commitment ledger

This is the "nothing slips" ledger. `/close-loops` scans your recent email and
meetings, finds commitments, and keeps this file current. `/morning-briefing`
and `/weekly-review` read it so open loops resurface until they're closed.

A commitment is anything that creates an expectation:
- **I owe** — you said you'd do/send/decide something.
- **Owed to me** — someone owes you something you're waiting on.

> The assistant maintains the tables below. When something is done, move it to
> the **Recently closed** section with a date rather than deleting it, so the
> weekly review can see what got finished.
